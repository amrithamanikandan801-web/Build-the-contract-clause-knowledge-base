DABA Day 4 – Contract-Clause Knowledge Base
Objective

The objective of Day 4 was to build a contract-clause knowledge base for supply-chain compliance analysis.

The knowledge base stores important procurement contract clauses, their requirements, risk levels, and corresponding compliance checks.

Created visualizations for delay and contract risk analysis.
Project Outcome

The Day 4 implementation provides a structured knowledge base that can be used by the supply-chain compliance workflow to identify contract requirements related to shipment delays and support automated compliance analysis.
